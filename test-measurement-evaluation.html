Test, Measurement & Evaluation: Complete Notes

This chapter covers the scientific foundation of physical education. Learn how to accurately assess performance, track progress, and make informed decisions using the core principles of testing, measurement, and evaluation.

Key Topics Covered

  • Defining the Terms: Clear definitions and differences between Test, Measurement, and Evaluation.
  • Importance and Principles: Why these concepts are crucial in sports, for talent identification, and for creating effective training programs.
  • Body Mass Index (BMI): The calculation and interpretation of BMI for assessing weight status.
  • Waist-Hip Ratio: How to calculate and use this ratio to assess body fat distribution and health risk.
